# AI Flywheel Standard Operating Procedure

All implementation work must occur through an approved mission and goal.

Each attempt to advance a goal must create an execution beneath that goal:

`.flywheel/operations/missions/<mission>/goals/<goal>/executions/<execution>/`

Executions preserve lifecycle evidence and failed attempts. Durable records may be promoted to `.flywheel/operations/records/`. Reusable knowledge may be promoted to `.flywheel/operations/knowledge/` only after validation.
